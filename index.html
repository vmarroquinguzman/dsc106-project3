01 / INTRODUCTION

Understanding California's Wildfires

Wildfires are an important environmental challenge in California. By examining historical burned area, aerosol optical depth, and land surface temperature, we aim to better understand patterns in wildfire activity and how they relate to environmental conditions.

OUR RESEARCH QUESTION

How have California's wildfire patterns changed from 2003 to 2025, and how are they associated with aerosol loading and land surface temperature?

05 / METHODOLOGY

Behind the Data

Our project uses NASA MODIS satellite observations to investigate California wildfire activity and associated environmental conditions.

Data Source

NASA MODIS satellite data from the Terra and Aqua missions.

Study Region

California, United States.

Time Period

January 2003 – December 2025.

Measurements

Burned area, aerosol optical depth, and land surface temperature.

Note: The visualizations examine associations rather than establishing causal relationships. Aerosol optical depth includes aerosols from sources other than wildfire smoke.
